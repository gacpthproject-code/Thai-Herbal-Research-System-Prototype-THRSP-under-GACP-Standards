Can an application reach certificate issuance with no cultivation type declared in its own data? (round 3, item 1)
Measured on real Postgres through the real applications router and the real certificate-service (scratch DB, synthetic data).
The draft: JURISTIC holder, plant cannabis, purpose RESEARCH, documents for the rules in force attached,
formData with no farmData.areaTypes, no cultivationMethods, no plots, no locationType; applications.areaType = OUTDOOR.

P0 = what deriveDimensions answers for the draft
P1 = POST /api/applications/submit with the canonical payload
P2 = POST /api/applications/submit with a legacy step-keyed payload (steps 1-9, validated by validateAllSteps)
P3 = generateCertificate for that application after onsite evidence (status set to AUDIT_PASSED directly)

Reading: the canonical door refuses (step 2 cultivation_methods). The legacy step-keyed door accepts it and mints the
quotation; issuance then records the column's OUTDOOR as the certificate's scope. The steps in between (payment, document
review, scheduling, inspection, approval) were not walked: every reader that can refuse on the area declaration goes
through resolveApplicationRequirements, which refuses only an unreadable declaration, and the column fills the ticks.
